Deal Lens Oman

A small tool I built for myself. I describe what I want in plain words, it searches AliExpress, and it shows me real listings with the price in OMR delivered to Oman.

What it does

  • Understands a vague request — "a bedside clock with a light I can switch off easily" becomes real product searches.
  • Shows real prices — each listing is opened and the actual price read, not the cheapest-variant teaser.
  • Oman first — prices in OMR, listings filtered to what actually ships to Oman.
  • Filterable — by price, by Choice items, by free shipping, and by rating.

How I use it

  • I type what I'm looking for.
  • I browse the results and compare prices.
  • When something is worth buying, I open it on AliExpress and order it normally.

Disclosure

  • Links to AliExpress in this tool are affiliate links.
  • If I buy through them, AliExpress may pay me a small commission at no extra cost to me.
  • It does not change the price shown, and it does not change which listings I see.
